Sam
   A Titan spawn known as Sam. Sam is incredibly well respected among the
Titans. His Patron is Hyperion (treats him with respect and he has been given
many gifts during his very long life.).
   SATT’s first recorded interaction with him was in 520 AD leading a
fighting force in what is now Germany.
   Likes honey cake, fine wines and Indian food. Dislikes daffodils raspberry
and orange.
   Sam lives at a villa in the south of France. It is built using modern
materials and techniques but in the Roman style. It is decorated with images
of Lancelot du Lac, Guinevere, Arthur and Roman Senators.
   He has a passion for history. The guest rooms feature old masters. The
library is full of leather bound books (many about history).
   The computer centre is a serious piece of work.
   His training room has scorched targets but no sign of projectile weapons.
There is also a suit of armour, with no livery.
   At the heart of the villa is a pool featuring a mosaic of Britannia with a
sundisc overlooking her.
   He has a London residence at 41 Gloucester Place.
   Sir. Gawain was a knight of the the Round Table.
   Due to their affair, he killed Sir Lamarok and Morgawse (although the
latter was secretly saved). Sir. Avalon tried to poison him in revenge.
   At the final battle he threw Excalibur into the lake, then met Lancelot and
got into a fight, which he lost. He cried out and was saved by the Titans who
earned his loyalty.
   He can probably be counted on to send aid against Ares and Ptah.

Heb-Sed Mace                                                See fig. 3 (p.13)
  A mace depicting Narmer’s Heb-Sed. It may be the first depiction of the

at SATT, this is a copy. The source is unknown and warrants further
investigation.

Shadow Hide
  You can hide unnoticed in any area of shadow big enough to hide your
body. Someone with super hearing or smell will be able to make a contested
roll to know that you are there.



The Kladenets, Sword of Darkness
  You can use the sword to cast an area of darkness. This can work in
conjunction with your shadow hide.



Blessing

2d6 until the blessing expires. Level affects range.



Create Ice

conditions.
